IPL AUCTION 2026
Rule Book
Budget and Team Formation Guidelines - Each team is given a limited budget of
100cr as purse money to build their squad.
- All teams are required to create a squad of 11 players with the following composition:
- 4 BOWLERS
- 4 to 5 BATSMEN
- 2 to 3 ALL-ROUNDERS
- 1 WICKET-KEEPER (must be included in the squad)
Evaluation Criteria - Evaluation will be based on the stats provided on the screen
Round 1: Team Formation -
Objective: Build your squad with a minimum of 11 players. - Once the team is formed, it will be
reviewed, and only qualified teams will move on to Round 2. Teams that do not succeed in
forming a team of 11 players will be eliminated immediately.
Mystery players Rule:-
We have introduced a fun surprise element here known as ”Mystery Players ”. The base
price of every mystery player may or may not be the same. The Auction of every mystery
player will be normal like any other IPL Player. The player and his stats will only be revealed
after a specific team will buy that player
Round 2: Captain and Vice-Captain Assignment - Objective: Assign a Captain and Vice-
Captain to your team.
- Captain earns 2x points.
- Vice-Captain earns 1.5x points.
- Final Step: - Submit your final playing 11 (with Captain and Vice-Captain) to the event
coordinators via the provided spreadsheet.
Additional Rules
Auction: - Only the Team Leader will be allowed to bid for players during the auction.
Electronic Gadgets: - All types of electronic or digital gadgets may be used during the event
for research or communication purposes.

INCREMENTS DURING BIDDING:
BELOW 1 CR- 10 LAKHS
BELOW 5 CR- 25 LAKHS
ABOVE 5 CR- 50 LAKHS
Disqualification:
• Any team which does not manage to form a squad of 11 players
will be disqualified.
• After forming the final squad, the team needs to submit the final
playing 11, failing to do the same will lead to disqualification.
• If any team member other than Team Leader is found to be bidding
for more than 3 times, the team will be disqualified.
• Any team found not abiding the Team Composition rule will be
disqualified.
• Any act of physical or verbal indiscipline between teams or with the
organizing team will lead to disqualification.
• Any kind of foul play cannot be tolerated.
• Any inappropriate or disrectpectful behavior will not be tolerated
and may result in removal from the event.
• If any team’s purse money is over and still that team bids on any
player, that team will be immediately disqualified.
*Any modifications in the rules will be informed to all participants
prior to the commencement of the event.
